# Pilot video deliverable

`output/follow-check-cartoon-story-v2.mp4` is the preferred post-ready Follow Detective video. It tells a four-scene mini story with a setup, surprise, investigation, and resolution.

`output/follow-check-pilot-v1.mp4` is retained as the earlier animated-poster cut.

- Canvas: 1080 × 1920 (9:16)
- Duration: 15 seconds
- Video: H.264
- Audio: AAC voiceover
- Captions: burned in
- Music: intentionally omitted so a current licensed sound can be added natively when posting

Suggested post caption and the broader content plan are in `../PILOT_CAMPAIGN.md`.

Before publishing, add a low-volume track from Instagram's or TikTok's licensed/commercial music library and preview the platform UI to confirm that no controls cover the caption text.
