# Follow Check cartoon campaign

## Campaign idea: The Follow Detective

A recurring check-mark detective investigates funny cases of one-way social follows. Each short starts with a relatable social-media mystery, reveals the private solution, and ends with one simple action: visit `follow-check.com`.

Core promise:

> Find the accounts that do not follow you back—without sharing your Instagram password. Your export stays on your device.

Keep every claim consistent with the product: Follow Check reads an official Instagram JSON export, does not connect to a live Instagram account, and costs $2.99 for one audit.

## Pilot video: The One-Way Follow

Format: 9:16 vertical, 15–18 seconds, 1080 × 1920, captions inside the platform safe area.

| Time | Visual/action | On-screen copy | Voiceover |

| 5–9s | Check Detective enters and examines the broken arrow. | `Case opened 🔍` | “Let the Follow Detective check.” |
| 9–13s | Quick crop toward the phone and detective. | `No password. Your file stays on your device.` | “No password, and your Instagram file never leaves your device.” |
| 13–17s | Clean end card using the cream background and green/red palette. | `Find the one-way follows.`<br>`follow-check.com · $2.99` | “Run your private Follow Check for two ninety-nine.” |

Editing notes:

- Animate the still with a slow 103–108% push-in, arrow wiggles, a snap sound at 3 seconds, and a magnifying-glass pop at 6 seconds.
- Use a human voiceover and platform-native captions. Keep the delivery conversational rather than announcer-like.
- Add a currently relevant sound from the platform’s commercial or licensed music library at low volume beneath the voiceover.
- Keep text away from the bottom and right-side interface areas.
- End with `Independent tool. Not affiliated with Instagram or Meta.` in small but readable text.

## Caption

Ever wonder how many follows are actually one-way? 👀 Follow Check compares your official Instagram export privately in your browser—no password and no subscription. Your file stays on your device.

Try it at follow-check.com

Independent tool. Not affiliated with Instagram or Meta.

## Initial content queue

1. **The One-Way Follow** — introduce the Check Detective and the core problem.
2. **The Group Chat Suspect** — three friends guess who quietly stopped following; reveal that guessing is unnecessary.
3. **The Password Trap** — detective blocks a suspicious “connect your account” door and points to the official-export route.
4. **The Celebrity Alibi** — show how users can exclude celebrities and brands from results.
5. **The All-Time Clue** — explain why selecting “All time” in the Instagram export matters.
6. **The $2.99 Case File** — contrast one small payment with another unwanted subscription.
7. **The File Never Leaves** — visualize the export staying safely inside the user’s phone/browser.
8. **Follower Spring Cleaning** — a satisfying before/after list-cleanup story without showing real usernames.
9. **Detective Answers Comments** — turn a real audience question into the opening hook.
10. **Everyone Follows Back** — a comedic rare-case celebration that keeps the brand from feeling negative.

## Four-week publishing test

- Publish three videos per week on both Instagram Reels and TikTok.
- Test three opening-hook families: curiosity (`Who actually follows back?`), humor (`The detective found a suspect`), and privacy (`Never hand an unfollower app your password`).
- Reuse the core cartoon but change the first two seconds, caption, voiceover, and end card so each post is a meaningful variant.
- Review results after every six posts. Optimize for three-second hold, average watch time, completion rate, profile visits, link clicks, and paid audits—not views alone.
- Continue the top two hooks and retire weak openings; do not judge the entire cartoon concept from one post.
