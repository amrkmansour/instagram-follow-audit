# People You Thought Would Follow Forever

A short-form animated dramedy about the strangely personal stories hiding inside a follower list. Each 15-second episode begins with a human relationship, earns a laugh or emotional turn, and reveals Follow Check only at the end.

## Format

- 15 seconds, 9:16 vertical, four illustrated story beats and a restrained end card.
- Dialogue and character sound lead; no sales narrator.
- Follow Check appears only in the final 2–3 seconds.
- The tone is observant and warm. Nobody is treated as a villain merely for no longer following someone.
- Stories may use new characters and visual styles; consistency comes from the title, emotional structure, and end card.

## Story structure

1. A specific relationship or shared history.
2. The discovery that the follow is now one-way.
3. A heightened comic reaction or imagined cutaway.
4. A grounded human resolution.
5. `Some mysteries are personal. Follow Check.`

## Product truth

Follow Check can show that an account in an official Instagram export is not following the user back. It cannot determine exactly when someone unfollowed, send live unfollow notifications, or prove the other person's motive. Scripts must respect that distinction.
