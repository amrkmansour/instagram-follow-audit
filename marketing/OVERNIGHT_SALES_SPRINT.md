## Ten-minute engagement window

Stay available for the first ten minutes after publishing. Reply like the creator of the story, not like a customer-support bot. Good reply patterns:

- `the vacation photos made it personal 😭`
- `Jordan chose peace after the tiny funeral`
- `okay but would YOU unfollow Cass back?`
- `episode 2 is going to be the work bestie`

Do not cold-DM, mass-comment, buy followers, or paste the product link under unrelated posts.

## Morning scorecard

Record, per platform:

- Views
- Average watch time or retention
- Completion rate
- Shares and saves
- Profile visits
- Website clicks
- Stripe completed checkout count and campaign metadata
- Completed audit events

The strongest signal is a completed purchase or audit. If there is no sale, keep the story and test a different first 1.5 seconds before abandoning the idea.
