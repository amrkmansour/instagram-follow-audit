# Follow Check TikTok Creator Marketing Report

Prepared August 3, 2026. Assumption: U.S., English-language campaign aimed primarily at college students and secondarily at college-bound users age 18+. Follower counts are research snapshots, not guarantees.

## Executive recommendation

Do not begin with a large creator or an expensive flat-fee campaign. Follow Check costs $2.99; after a typical Stripe domestic-card fee of 2.9% + $0.30, approximately $2.60 remains before refunds and taxes. A $500 post therefore needs about 193 purchases merely to recover the creator fee.

Run a three-creator pilot among accounts near 50K–100K followers. Offer a modest guaranteed payment plus a per-sale commission, use a unique tracked link for each creator, and scale only creators producing purchases at an acceptable cost. Prioritize college audiences age 18+ rather than marketing a paid product directly to minors.

## Economics and offer structure

Published 2026 guides vary widely, but place a single TikTok from a 50K–250K creator around $600–$2,500; usage rights and Spark Ads commonly cost extra. This is directionally consistent across the [P3RSON 2026 rate guide](https://p3rson.com/content/rate-guide-2026.pdf) and [SponsorRate](https://sponsorrate.com/blog/tiktok-rates/). Actual pricing should be based on median recent views, not follower count.

| Creator cost | Purchases needed to recover fee at ~$2.60 net |
|---:|---:|
| $100 | 39 |
| $250 | 97 |
| $500 | 193 |
| $1,000 | 385 |
| $2,500 | 962 |

Recommended pilot:

- Three creators in the 50K–100K band.
- Ask for one 20–30 second TikTok, a 48-hour bio link, and performance screenshots after 7 and 14 days.
- Opening offer: $100–$200 guaranteed plus $0.60 per verified purchase, capped for the pilot. Expect some creators to decline; do not exceed $250 flat unless their recent median views and audience data justify it.
- Do not initially purchase exclusivity, perpetual usage, or Spark Ads rights. Negotiate those only for a winning video.
- Pilot budget: $450–$750 total plus capped commission. Stop or change the product economics if the cost per purchase stays above approximately $2.60.

The practical alternative is raising the price, selling a multi-audit pass, or using a creator video as reusable ad creative. At the present price, influencer marketing is more likely to be customer research than immediate profit.

## Required creator verification

Before paying, request screenshots from TikTok analytics showing:

- Last 10 non-sponsored posts and their median views (not the single best viral post).
- Audience age, with a strong preference for 18–24; do not target minors directly.
- Audience geography, preferably majority United States for this campaign.
- Average watch time and completion rate on comparable videos.
- Prior sponsored-post results, if available.
- Their exact quote, revisions, posting window, content ownership and usage-rights terms.

Reject creators with unexplained follower spikes, repetitive/bot-like comments, a low recent-view median, an audience concentrated outside the target market, or excessive sponsored content.

## Creative brief

Suggested concept: “I’m following 1,247 people—here’s how I found who doesn’t follow me back without giving an app my Instagram password.”

1. Show the problem in the first three seconds.
2. Show the official Instagram information export.
3. Show the local upload and result; use a genuine audit, not a simulated testimonial.
4. State the two strongest benefits: no Instagram login and files stay on the user’s device.
5. End with “It costs $2.99 at follow-check.com.”

The creator must clearly say or display “Ad”/“Sponsored” in the video and enable TikTok’s paid-content disclosure. The FTC says disclosure must be hard to miss and in the video—not merely buried in the description—and creators cannot claim an experience they did not have. See the [FTC disclosure guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers) and [TikTok branded-content instructions](https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-and-business-accounts/promoting-a-brand-product-or-service).

>
> Are you open to one paid 20–30 second TikTok plus a 48-hour bio link? Please send your quote and screenshots of your last-10-post median views, audience age, and U.S. audience percentage. We would provide a unique tracked link and pay a guaranteed fee plus commission on verified sales. The post would use TikTok's Paid Partnership disclosure.

Personalize the first sentence. Do not mass-send identical messages or pretend to be a fan.

## Tracking and launch checklist

Before outreach becomes paid placement, Follow Check should have:

- Working HTTPS on `follow-check.com` and a complete purchase-to-audit test on mobile.
- The public/shared access-code bypass removed or replaced with a private administrative mechanism.
- Cloudflare Web Analytics or equivalent privacy-conscious analytics.
- Unique creator URLs such as `https://follow-check.com/?ref=jendasom&utm_source=tiktok&utm_campaign=pilot1`.
- The referral value stored in Stripe Checkout metadata so revenue can be attributed after payment.
- Purchase, checkout-start, checkout-complete and audit-complete measurement.
- Visible privacy, refund/contact and terms information.

Do not pay creators until these checks pass. Follower counts and third-party engagement figures should be refreshed on the day of outreach and replaced by native TikTok analytics before signing.

## Exact pre-advertising launch sequence

Complete these in order. A box should be checked only when evidence is saved in a campaign folder.

### Phase 1 — Fix and verify the product

- [ ] Deploy the $2.99 Stripe Price and Worker verification change together. Never deploy the webpage price alone.
- [ ] Change `APP_URL` and allowed origins to HTTPS only after the certificate is active; remove the HTTP origin.
- [ ] Run a real $2.99 mobile purchase using a card, Apple Pay or Google Pay when available; verify the audit unlocks exactly once.
- [ ] Refund that test purchase in Stripe and save the receipt/refund evidence.
- [ ] Test checkout cancellation, refresh-after-payment, reused Checkout Session, incorrect export, oversized ZIP and expired/invalid proof.
- [ ] Test on current iPhone Safari, Android Chrome and desktop Chrome/Safari.
- [ ] Confirm the displayed price, Stripe Checkout price, charged amount and redemption amount check all say $2.99/299 cents.
- [ ] Confirm uploaded Instagram files, usernames and results never leave the device using browser network tools.
- [ ] Remove the shared `amr` customer-facing bypass. Replace it with a long private admin secret and never give it to creators or customers.
- [ ] Rotate any Stripe secret ever pasted into chat or exposed elsewhere, then store it only as a Cloudflare secret.

### Phase 2 — Establish trust and support

- [ ] Publish a plain-language Privacy Policy explaining local file processing, payment data handled by Stripe, analytics, retention and contact rights.
- [ ] Publish Terms covering one-audit access, prohibited abuse, Instagram/Meta non-affiliation and limitation of liability.
- [ ] Publish a refund policy that states when a failed audit qualifies and how to request help.
- [ ] Add a working support email on every policy page and test that messages are received.
- [ ] Add a short FAQ: what export to request, supported JSON/ZIP files, no Instagram password, local processing, price and typical completion time.
- [ ] Ensure all marketing claims are provable. Avoid “100% safe,” “Instagram approved,” or guaranteed accuracy.

### Phase 3 — Add attribution before contacting creators

- [ ] Preserve `ref`, `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` and `utm_content` from landing through Checkout.
- [ ] Put the creator reference into Stripe Checkout metadata; validate it against a short allowlist and never trust arbitrary metadata for entitlement.
- [ ] Record privacy-conscious events: landing view, checkout start, checkout success, audit start, audit success and audit failure.
- [ ] Give every creator one unique URL and code, for example `?ref=jendasom&utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=creator&utm_campaign=pilot_01`.
- [ ] Build a campaign sheet with creator, URL, post date, fee, commission, views, clicks, checkouts, purchases, refunds, revenue and CPA.
- [ ] Exclude the owner's tests and creator preview sessions from campaign results.
- [ ] Verify attribution end-to-end with a test referral and Stripe metadata before outreach.

### Phase 4 — Vet and contract creators

- [ ] Start with the top 15; do not message all 50 at once.
- [ ] Obtain native TikTok screenshots for last-10-post views, audience age, audience country, watch time and completion rate.
- [ ] Calculate the median of the last 10 non-sponsored videos. Reject based on median performance, not follower count alone.
- [ ] Require a predominantly 18–24 audience. Do not commission direct sales messaging to minors.
- [ ] Review the last 30 posts for unsafe conduct, harassment, hateful content, fake engagement, competitor deals and excessive sponsorships.
- [ ] Search each creator and handle for controversies and confirm the account is active and authentic.
- [ ] Get a written quote that separates organic post, revisions, bio link, usage rights, exclusivity and Spark Ads rights.
- [ ] Sign a simple agreement covering deliverables, deadline, factual claims, FTC/TikTok disclosure, approval, analytics delivery, payment timing, cancellation and content usage.
- [ ] Pay by traceable business method; never send gift cards, crypto or friends-and-family payment.

### Phase 5 — Produce and approve the advertisement

- [ ] Give the creator a real complimentary audit through a private single-use mechanism, not the shared password.
- [ ] Require them to use the product before endorsing it.
- [ ] Approve the concept and factual talking points, but let the creator use their normal voice.
- [ ] Show “Ad” or “Sponsored” in the video and spoken disclosure near the beginning; also enable TikTok's Paid Partnership disclosure.
- [ ] Demonstrate the official Instagram export and local upload without exposing real private usernames.
- [ ] State the price clearly as $2.99 and disclose that Follow Check is independent from Instagram/Meta.
- [ ] Verify the unique referral URL before the post goes live.
- [ ] Save the final approved video, caption, disclosure and agreement.

### Phase 6 — Pilot, measure and decide

- [ ] Launch only three creators in the first pilot, staggered by at least three days.
- [ ] Cap the pilot at $450–$750 guaranteed fees plus capped commissions.
- [ ] Capture results at 24 hours, 72 hours, seven days and fourteen days.
- [ ] Primary metric: cost per completed paid audit. Secondary metrics: click-through, checkout conversion, refund rate and audit failure rate.
- [ ] Do not scale a creator whose CPA exceeds approximately $2.60 unless the campaign is explicitly being treated as paid research.
- [ ] Interview or survey early purchasers about trust, price and why they bought before raising spend.
- [ ] Scale only a winning creator or creative; negotiate Spark Ads rights after organic proof, not before.
- [ ] Stop immediately for broken checkout, misleading claims, missing disclosures, high failure/refund rates or hostile audience response.
