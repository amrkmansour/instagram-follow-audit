Instagram export

How to download your Instagram followers and following data

Request only the connection data you need, export it to your device, and use the All time and JSON settings. Those choices are essential for a reliable comparison.

Published and last reviewed: August 10, 2026

  1. Open Accounts Center

    Go to Your information and permissions in Accounts Center. Sign in directly with Instagram or Meta—never through an unfollower tool.

  2. Choose Export your information

    Select Export your information, create a new export, choose the Instagram profile, and export to your device.

  3. Customize the information

    Clear the other categories. Under Connections, leave only Followers and following selected.

  4. Choose the required settings

    Set the date range to All time, format to JSON, and keep medium media quality. Media quality does not affect the username lists.

  5. Start and download the export

    Instagram will notify you when the archive is available. Download the original ZIP and keep it private.

What files should be inside?

The relevant folder normally contains following.json and one or more files named followers_1.json, followers_2.json, and so on. Larger accounts may have multiple numbered followers files. Include all of them.

Common mistakes

  • Using a limited date range: the export may omit older relationships and produce misleading results.
  • Choosing HTML: HTML is useful for browsing but is not the structured format accepted by FollowCheck.
  • Selecting only one numbered file: every followers_N.json file is part of the list.
  • Uploading the archive to an unknown converter: an Instagram export can contain sensitive account information.
Protect the ZIP

Use it only with software whose processing you understand. FollowCheck reads the required files locally in your browser and does not upload them.
